Asian Styled Rice and Eggs

Description

Even though it is named asian styled rice and rggs its not that different then other simple rice and eggs recipes that you can find. I alwaysed love eating this as a kid since it is super easy to make, it only takes about 5 minutes, and tastes amazing.

Actual Recipes

First you will need a couple things.

Ingredient Amount
Already Cooked Rice About 1/2 cup
Eggs 2
Soy Sauce 2 Tablespoons

Instructions

  1. Put the pan on the stove and turn it to high
  2. Wait for the pan to get hot and then add eggs
  3. Scramble the eggs until cooked
  4. Add Rice and mix with the srambled eggs
  5. Put the rice and egg in the bowl
  6. Add Soy Sauce and mix well
  7. Enjoy!
