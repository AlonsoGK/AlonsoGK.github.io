Duck's Tacos

Welcome to our website. We make the best tacos in all of New York City, come try some during your lunch break so you can taste it for yourself. We are located in Times Square with a big sign over our stall.

Pollo Asado

Pollo Asado is a Mexican dish that has juicy chicken with a slightly charred exterior. It also lime juice, orange juice and some white vingar to give it its delious taste. Served with rice. 🦆

Carne Asada

Carne Asada literally means grilled meat in Spanish. It is one of the most popular dishes from Mexico. It is made of steak that is marinated and grilled over high heat. Served with rice. 🦆

Old School Beef Tacos

Old School Beef Tacos are traditional Mexican-styled tacos. Made of hard shells, beef and onions, cheese, black beans, lttuce and our special house sauce.🦆
