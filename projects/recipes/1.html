Classic Chicken

Description

Classic Chicken is a simple meal made with ground chicken, rice, and a few other things for flavor. The recipe doesn't go into how to make rice, focuses on the chicken.

Actual Recipe

First you are going to need a few things.

Instructions

  1. Take the meat and put it into a bowl
  2. Pour the soy sauce, salt, and peper in and mix well
  3. Mince the garlic
  4. Take the meat and fry it in a pan on high heat
  5. Cook until all the mat is dark and there is no pink left
  6. Add the garlic and mix well
  7. You're Done! Serve over rice

Tips, Tricks and subtitutions

  • Brfore adding the Meat wait for the pan to heat up on the stove for a bit
  • If you dont have Garlic cloves then you can use garlic powder
  • This recipe works with other types of meat such as beef, turkey, pork and more

Ingredient Amount
Ground Chicken 1 lb
Soy Sauce 1-3 tablespoons
Garlic 2 Cloves
Salt 3/4 teaspoon
Peper 1/4 Teaspoon
